# Surface: Essay writing desk (single page, five sections)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Diego's B1+ UTP students, mostly on phones, drafting one essay section at a time or the whole essay, as optional self-study. Task: write each part, check coherence, get marked with Diego's rubric, revise. Constraint: level and points first after marking; must stay easy to read on a phone and must not look generic. Visual world is inherited from the sibling app `C:\Dev\response_paragraph` (DESIGN.md "The Marked Notebook Sheet"), pinned by Diego's reference screenshot.
